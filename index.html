Case 1: Scaling a UK e-commerce Meta account on a warm audience

UK | Meta Ads | Jan to Oct 2026

Challenge

The brand already had strong organic and returning-customer demand. Spending heavily into the same audience pool raised frequency, and awareness campaigns added cost without meaningful new reach. Performance also followed a seasonal pattern that needed its own baseline.

Actions

  • Reviewed awareness and ThruPlay campaigns and kept them out of the performance view.
  • Excluded repeat customers from prospecting audiences to reduce wasted impressions and bring frequency down.
  • Concentrated budget on broad catalogue and lookalike campaigns, which drove most purchases.
  • Tracked frequency, CPC, CPM, and ROAS monthly to separate seasonal lift from structural change.

Outcomes

Case 2: Rebuilding a German Meta account and lowering cost per purchase

DE | Meta Ads | Jan to Oct 2026

Challenge

The German account had low traffic and inconsistent results. Spring spend was high, ROAS fell below 2, and frequency rose in the higher-spend months. Campaign structure had grown fragmented, which made the performance picture hard to read.

Actions

  • Judged performance on rolling three-month averages, since monthly purchase counts were small.
  • Reduced spend and consolidated campaigns around the strongest audiences.
  • Monitored a small, high-frequency add-to-cart audience separately, with exclusions and creative refresh planned.
  • Reported Germany separately from wider European campaigns so the baseline stayed clear.

Outcomes

Case 3: Attribution review for duplicated conversions across Meta and Google

Meta + Google Ads | Attribution

Challenge

Meta and Google Ads were both claiming the same purchases, which inflated reported performance and made true sales growth hard to judge. Meta spend also showed delivery gaps from payment issues, which needed to be read separately from performance.

Actions

  • Changed the Meta attribution setting to engagement and click, reducing double-counting with Google Ads.
  • Recognised that the change lowers reported Meta purchases, so Meta results are read as a relative trend rather than a full sales total.
  • Identified payment interruptions as a source of delivery gaps and planned to note them alongside monthly spend.

Outcomes

Lower
Reported Meta purchases after the change (less double-counting)
Next step
Reconcile Meta and Google totals against website orders

Case 4: Lead generation for a local service business, Sri Lanka

Meta Ads | Lead Forms + Messaging | 2026

Challenge

A local service business needed a steady flow of enquiries from a small market, where audience size limits how far cost can be pushed down. Lead-form costs varied widely between campaigns, so the question was which creative and audience to keep.

Actions

  • Compared lead-form and messaging campaigns on cost per result rather than volume alone.
  • Moved budget toward the lowest-cost messaging creative, which cost about one-third as much per conversation as the weakest messaging campaign.
  • Flagged the highest-cost lead-form campaign for creative and audience review.

Outcomes

~1/3

Case 5: Traffic and messaging campaigns for a local business, 2025

Meta Ads | Traffic + Messaging | 2025

Challenge

The account ran several objectives at once: link traffic, messaging, and reach. Blended cost figures would have hidden the real performance of each goal, so each needed to be judged separately.

Actions

  • Reported each objective separately instead of blending traffic, messaging, and reach into one cost figure.
  • Compared cost per link click across campaigns and kept the lowest-cost traffic setups running.
  • Used low frequency as a signal that the audience had room to scale before fatigue became a risk.

Outcomes

~1/3

How I Work

A repeatable approach I use on every account.

Audit the account

Check structure, audience overlap, attribution settings, and tracking before changing any budget.

Set a baseline

Compare against seasonal norms and rolling averages so changes aren't misread.

Act on signals

Read frequency, cost per result, and CTR trends together, not any single metric alone.

Document and reconcile

Log attribution and campaign changes alongside the data, and reconcile platform totals against actual orders or leads.
